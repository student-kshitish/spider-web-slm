What this experiment is
-----------------------
Clean A/B testing whether a STRUCTURAL (direction-gated) retrieval constraint
helps or hurts cross-sentence binding, on top of the position-resolved memory
fix. Both arms use the new StructuralOrbitalRead (core/memory.py), wired in
core/web.py after the hop loop, toggled by config use_struct_read/struct_mask.

  Arm A (struct_on)  : inward structural mask ON  (struct_mask=True)
                       a query at ring r may read only positions whose ring is
                       inward-or-equal (ring[s] <= ring[t]), plus causal s<=t.
  Arm B (struct_off) : FLAT causal read           (struct_mask=False)
                       any causal position s<=t, no ring restriction.
  They differ ONLY by the structural mask.

KEY FIX (vs the failed query-read run): memory is now POSITION-RESOLVED. The
read's bank is the per-position hidden states (full T=256, NO mean-pool over
time), so retrieval can reach a SPECIFIC earlier entity. This solves the
time-collapse bug that made the previous slot-read unable to bind. Confirmed
working: lookback ~0.96-0.99 (most attention mass goes to past positions, not
self). The read is causal (verified: zero attention mass above the diagonal).
